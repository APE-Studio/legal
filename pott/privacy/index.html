Pott is a fines and kitty ledger for amateur sports teams. The team's treasurer keeps the list of fines, dues, payments and expenses on an iPhone; players see their own balance through a web link or in the app.

The short version: Pott never holds, collects or moves money. It has no payment buttons, asks for no bank details and links to no payment app. "Cash", "Tikkie" and "Bank" are only labels the treasurer puts on an entry. What Pott stores is the team's ledger, so the treasurer can keep it on more than one phone and players can see their own part. There is no advertising and no tracking, and we sell nothing to anyone.

Who is who

  • The treasurer sets up the team and signs in with Apple. This is the only account in Pott.
  • Players never make an account. They open the team's link in a browser, or join in the Pott app by picking their name.

The team's ledger

When the treasurer saves the team, the app sends it to our database so it is safe, works on a second phone and can be shown to players. It holds:

  • the team name, club name (if given), sport, colour and season;
  • player names, as the treasurer types them (a first name or nickname is enough), and whether a player is hidden or on the waiting list;
  • the team's fines list (names and amounts);
  • every entry: fines, dues, payments (with the label cash, Tikkie, bank or other), expenses (with the description and category the treasurer types), and corrections. Entries are never changed afterwards; a correction is a new entry, so the ledger stays honest;
  • "I paid" messages from players: which player, the amount they say they paid (all that was open, half, or another amount) and the label they chose;
  • join requests: a player who is not on the team's list can write their own name and ask to join. The name they write is stored with the team. The treasurer approves it (as that name, as a nickname, or as a player already on the list) or declines it. After approval the written name stays on that player, so the player sees what they signed up as. Requests, approved or declined, are deleted with the team;
  • the player link and team code, which the treasurer can replace at any time (the old link then stops working).

A receipt photo added to an expense stays on the phone; it is not uploaded. The phone also keeps its own copy of the team, so the app works offline and sends what is waiting when it is back online.

Player names belong to the team. The treasurer decides which names go in and can rename or hide a player at any time. A player who wants their name changed or removed can ask the treasurer, or write to us and we will replace it.

Sign in with Apple (treasurer)

The treasurer signs in with Apple when the team is first saved. Apple gives us a stable user ID for the account and, the first time, the first name the treasurer chooses to share. Pott does not ask Apple for the email address. The first name is stored with the team and shown to players ("Pay Mark the usual way"). The sign-in session is kept in the iPhone's Keychain.

Players

The web link (apestudios.app/pott/#…): the team's link code sits after the # in the address, so it never reaches a server log. The page asks our server for that team's view, shows the player only their own balance and items, plus the team's pot and what it was spent on (never another player's amount), and remembers the last view and the chosen name in the browser's local storage on that device. "I paid" sends a message to the treasurer; it books nothing. No cookies, no account, no tracking.

The app: a player who joins in the Pott app gets a random, anonymous ID from our database, bound to the name they pick. If they say yes to notifications, the phone's Apple push token is stored with that ID. The camera is used only to scan the team's QR code, and the image is not kept. The treasurer can undo a join at any time.

Not on the list: on the web link or in the app, a player whose name is missing can write it and wait for the treasurer. The page or app keeps a random code for that request on the device, so it can check whether the treasurer has answered.

How the app is used (app events)

The app sends short records of what happens in it to our database, in small batches (they wait on the phone while it is offline). Each record holds:

  • What happened: for example "opened Home", "went to the next onboarding step", "finished onboarding with 18 players", "gave a fine to 2 players", "recorded a payment (label Tikkie)", "recorded an expense (with or without receipt)", "shared the player link", "opened or closed the subscription screen", "started a purchase or trial", "tapped Restore", "a player joined", "a player said they paid", "answered the notification question", "deleted the account". Only names of screens and steps, counts and labels like these.
  • Technical context: the app version and build, the iOS version, the iPhone model, the time, and two random IDs the app makes itself: one for this install and one for each time the app is opened.

These records never contain a name, an amount, a team, a player or an account ID, or anything typed in. The install ID is not linked to the treasurer's account, to a team or to RevenueCat's ID, and a reinstall makes a new one. Our server sees the IP address any internet request carries; it is not stored with the records.

Why: to find problems and see which parts of Pott are used, so we know what to improve. Not for advertising, not sold, not combined with other companies' data. How long: two years, then they are deleted automatically.

Subscriptions (Team Pro, Club)

Team Pro and Club are optional. Apple takes the payment through the Apple ID; we never see a card or the Apple ID. To show prices, check whether a plan is active and restore it, the app uses RevenueCat, which acts on our behalf. RevenueCat receives a random ID the app creates, the app version, device model, iOS version, language, App Store country and currency, the IP address any request carries, and, when a plan is bought or restored, Apple's purchase record (plan, price, trial and renewal dates, transaction IDs). When the treasurer buys a plan, the team's ID is added so the plan follows the team. Used only to unlock and restore plans and for our sales totals. RevenueCat's policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/.

Deleting your account

Treasurer: Pott → Settings → Delete account. This deletes, at once and for everyone, your account and every team you own: the players, the fines list, every entry and every "I paid" message. The player link and team code stop working, and the app on your phone starts over as new. Database backups kept by our host roll over and no longer contain it within 30 days. Deleting the account does not cancel a subscription; Apple holds it, so cancel it in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. The subscription record at RevenueCat is deleted on request (see Your rights).

Player: deleting the app removes the player's view from the phone; the treasurer can undo the join, which unbinds the anonymous ID from the name. The player's entries stay in the team's ledger, because they are the team's records.

Without deletion, the team's data stays as long as the treasurer keeps the account.

Our processors: Supabase stores the database in the EU (Frankfurt); RevenueCat processes the subscription record in the United States under its data processing terms and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses; Apple provides Sign in with Apple, push notifications and the App Store.

Legal bases: for the treasurer's account and the team's ledger, performing the service the treasurer asked for (contract). For app events, our legitimate interest in fixing and improving the app, with records that carry no name or amount. You may object, ask for access, correction or deletion, and complain to your data protection authority (in the Netherlands: the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

What Pott deliberately does not do

  • No money. No payments, no payment links, no IBAN, no bank connection.
  • No email address. Not asked from Apple, not asked from players.
  • No player accounts. No sign-up, no password, no profile.
  • No other player's amounts on a player's link or in a player's app.
  • No advertising, no tracking, no advertising ID, no data brokers.
  • No location, contacts or photo library access. A receipt is picked with the iOS photo picker, which needs no permission, and stays on the phone.

Permissions

  • Camera: only when a player taps to scan the team's QR code.
  • Notifications: asked in context (a player who wants a heads-up on a new fine; a treasurer whose free trial is about to end). Declining changes nothing else.

Children

The treasurer must be an adult. Pott is not directed at children. A youth team's treasurer may enter young players' first names; Pott asks for nothing else about them, and players need no account.

Your rights

Write to apestudios.io@gmail.com to see, correct or delete what we hold. A treasurer can delete everything in the app. For a player name, tell us the team and the name. For the subscription record, send the order ID from Apple's receipt email and we will ask RevenueCat to show or delete it. App events are not linked to anyone, so we cannot find a person's events; a reinstall starts a new random ID.
