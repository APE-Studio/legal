Support

Pott — APE Studios

Questions, a bug, or something that doesn't add up? Write to apestudios.io@gmail.com. A person reads every message, usually within two working days. Please mention your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

For the treasurer

  • Pott never handles money. Players pay you the usual way (cash, Tikkie, bank); you record the payment in Pott.
  • A wrong entry? Tap Undo on the message that appears right after booking. Booked entries are never edited later, so the history stays honest.
  • The link went to the wrong people? Settings → Player link → Make a new link. The old link stops working at once.
  • New phone? Install Pott and sign in with the same Apple ID; the team comes back from the server.
  • Delete everything: Settings → Delete account. This deletes your account and the team, for every player, and cannot be undone.

For players

  • No account needed. Open the link from your treasurer and pick your name, or join in the Pott app.
  • "I paid" tells the treasurer to check. Your balance changes once they record the payment.
  • Your name is wrong or should go? Ask your treasurer, or write to us with the team and the name.

Team Pro and Club

  • Cancel: on your iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Pott, or Pott → Settings → Manage subscription. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel it.
  • Restore on a new iPhone: same Apple ID, then Pott → Settings → Restore purchases.
  • Refund: Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Free trial: Pott reminds you two days before it ends; cancel before then if you don't want to pay.

Report something

An abusive name, fine or description in a team? Write to apestudios.io@gmail.com with the team name, and we will look at it.
