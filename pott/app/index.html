Pott: Team Kitty & Fines

The team kitty, without the spreadsheet. The treasurer books a fine in two taps, and every player sees their own balance through a link.

  • Your team’s own fines list, ready in one tap
  • Players need no app and no account
  • Pott never touches the money

What Pott does

Pott is a ledger for the team kitty of an amateur sports team. The treasurer keeps the team’s fines list, books fines, dues and expenses, and marks payments when they come in.

Each player opens a private link and sees what they owe, what the pot holds and where the money went. Players who want the app can join with the link, a QR code or the team code.

At the end of the season Pott names the fine king and makes a card to share with the team.

Questions

Does Pott collect the money?

No. Players pay the way the team already does, in cash or by bank transfer. The treasurer records the payment in Pott.

Do players need an account?

No. Only the treasurer signs in, with Apple. Players use the link or join in the app without an account.

What does it cost?

Free for one team of up to ten players. Team Pro removes the player limit for a yearly price per team, with a two-week free trial.

When is it out?

Soon, on iPhone, starting in the Netherlands.
