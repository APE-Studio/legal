Pott is a ledger, not a bank

This is the most important section on this page.

  • Pott never holds, collects or moves money. Players pay the treasurer outside the app, in cash, by Tikkie, by bank transfer or however the team agrees. "Cash", "Tikkie" and "Bank" are labels only.
  • The numbers are what the treasurer entered. Pott adds them up; it does not check them against any bank account. If a balance looks wrong, the treasurer's records and the team's agreement decide, not the app.
  • "I paid" is a message, not a payment. It tells the treasurer to check; only the treasurer books a payment.
  • Fines are the team's own agreement. The team decides what it fines and what it does with the pot. Pott is not party to that, and the team's own rules (and the law) apply.
