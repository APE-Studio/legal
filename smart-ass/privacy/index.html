Smart Ass is a music companion for the car: it plays the Top 100 songs of each decade from Apple Music, and a host tells you each song's story (Info) or quizzes you on it (Game). This policy describes what the app does with your data.

The short version: the app records how it is used (which screens you open, which songs play, how a game went, and errors) under a random ID that is not linked to you, so we can fix problems and improve the app. It never sends your name, your email or your Apple ID with that. Signing in is optional, and what signing in creates stays on your phone. There are no ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no data brokers.

How the app is used (app events)

While you use Smart Ass, the app writes short records of what happens in it and sends them, in small batches, to our database. If you are offline they wait on the phone and are sent later. Each record holds:

  • What happened: for example "opened the Game", "song started", "skipped to the next song", "muted the host", "answered round 3, correct, in 4 seconds", "game finished with 1,850 points", the decades you picked, and whether a song played in full or as a 30-second preview and why.
  • Errors: where something went wrong in the app and the error message (for example a song that failed to load or a download that did not finish).
  • Technical context: the app version and build, the iOS version, the iPhone model, the time, whether your Apple Music account can play full songs, and two random IDs the app makes itself: one for this install of the app and one for each time you open it.

These records never contain your name, your email address, your Apple ID, your leaderboard name, your location, your contacts or anything you type. The random install ID is not linked to your account or to any other ID, and it changes if you delete and reinstall the app. Like any request on the internet, sending them shows our server your IP address; we do not store it with the records.

Why: to find and fix problems (for example why a phone plays previews instead of full songs) and to see which parts of the app people use, so we can improve it. We do not use them for advertising, we do not sell them, and we do not combine them with data from other companies.

How long: up to two years, then they are deleted.

Apple Music

Songs play through Apple Music on your iPhone (Apple's MusicKit). When you tap "Connect Apple Music", iOS asks whether Smart Ass may use Apple Music. If you allow it, the app can play songs from Apple's catalogue and check whether your Apple Music subscription plays full songs. Without a subscription, or if you say no, the app plays Apple's 30-second previews and shows album covers from Apple. Apple handles all of this under Apple's privacy policy; the app does not read your library, your playlists or your listening history, and it does not change anything in your Apple Music account.

Signing in (optional)

You can play without an account. If you choose Sign in with Apple to keep your points, Apple gives the app an ID for your account and, the first time only and if you agree, your name. The app keeps these, your leaderboard name and your total points on your iPhone only (in the iOS Keychain). They are not sent to us. We never see your password or your email address.

You can sign out or delete your account in the app: Settings → Player → Delete account. That removes your name, leaderboard name and points from the phone at once.

If we later put the leaderboard online, we will update this policy before any of this leaves your phone.

Song packs

The host's voice for each decade is a download the app requests from Apple's App Store servers the first time you play that decade. Apple handles that download; it carries nothing about you.

Purchases

Any purchase in Smart Ass goes through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment: we never see or store your payment details. Cancelling works the way every App Store subscription does, in Settings under your name → Subscriptions.

What we deliberately do not collect

  • No tracking. No advertising ID, no ads, no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No location, microphone, camera, contacts or photos. The host talks; the app does not listen.
  • No names or emails in our database.

Third parties

  • Apple: Apple Music playback, previews and covers, Sign in with Apple, song pack downloads and App Store payments, as described above. Governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Supabase (supabase.com): hosts our database, where the app events are stored. It stores them on our behalf and does not use them for its own purposes.

Children

Smart Ass is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

The app events are not linked to you, so we cannot find yours on request; deleting and reinstalling the app starts a new random ID with no link to the old one. Your account data lives only on your phone, and you can delete it in the app at any time. For any question about your data, write to apestudios.io@gmail.com.
