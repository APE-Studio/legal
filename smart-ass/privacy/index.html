Privacy Policy

Smart Ass — APE Studios

Effective date: 23 November 2026
Developer: APE Studios
Contact: apestudios.io@gmail.com
Published at: https://apestudios.app/smart-ass/privacy/

Smart Ass is a music companion for the car: it plays the Top 100 songs of each decade from Apple Music, and a host tells you each song's story (Info) or quizzes you on it (Game). This policy describes what the app does with your data.

The short version: the app records how it is used (which screens you open, which songs play, how a game went, and errors) under a random ID that is not linked to you, so we can fix problems and improve the app. Those records never include your name, your email, your Apple ID or your nickname. Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Apple, we store a profile under your nickname, and each finished one-decade game, on our database. Smart Ass Pro is an App Store subscription: Apple takes the payment, and RevenueCat stores whether Pro is active. We never see your card. There are no ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no data brokers.

How the app is used (app events)

While you use Smart Ass, the app writes short records of what happens in it and sends them, in small batches, to our database. If you are offline they wait on the phone and are sent later. Each record holds:

  • What happened: for example "opened the Game", "song started", "skipped to the next song", "muted the host", "answered round 3, correct, in 4 seconds", "game finished with 1,850 points", the decades you picked, and whether a song played in full or as a 30-second preview and why.
  • Errors: where something went wrong in the app and the error message (for example a song that failed to load or a download that did not finish).
  • Technical context: the app version and build, the iOS version, the iPhone model, the time, whether your Apple Music account can play full songs, and two random IDs the app makes itself: one for this install of the app and one for each time you open it.

These records never contain your name, your email address, your Apple ID, your nickname, your location, your contacts or anything you type. The random install ID is not linked to your account or to any other ID, and it changes if you delete and reinstall the app. Like any request on the internet, sending them shows our server your IP address; we do not store it with the records.

Why: to find and fix problems (for example why a phone plays previews instead of full songs) and to see which parts of the app people use, so we can improve it. We do not use them for advertising, we do not sell them, and we do not combine them with data from other companies.

How long: up to two years, then they are deleted.

Apple Music

Songs play through Apple Music on your iPhone (Apple's MusicKit). When you tap "Connect Apple Music", iOS asks whether Smart Ass may use Apple Music. If you allow it, the app can play songs from Apple's catalogue and check whether your Apple Music subscription plays full songs. Without a subscription, or if you say no, the app plays Apple's 30-second previews and shows album covers from Apple. Apple handles all of this under Apple's privacy policy; the app does not read your library, your playlists or your listening history, and it does not change anything in your Apple Music account.

Signing in (optional)

You can play without an account. If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple gives the app a sign-in token and, the first time only and if you agree, your name. The app sends that token to our database (Supabase) and creates a profile there. The profile holds your nickname: it starts from the name Apple gave you, and you can change it in Settings. We never see your password, and we do not ask Apple for your email.

The name Apple gave you, your nickname and your total points are also kept on this iPhone (in the iOS Keychain), so the app can show them without waiting for the network.

A finished game on one decade is stored with that profile: the decade, the score, how many you got right, and each answer (what you picked, or that the time ran out, and how long you took). The app has no leaderboard yet. If one is added, it will show your nickname, the decade, the score and how many you got right. The answers themselves stay with your account. A game that mixes decades is not stored, and neither is a game you play while signed out. Those still count on the phone only.

You can sign out in Settings → Player. Sign out ends the session. The profile and the games stay until you delete the account.

Delete account is in Settings → Player. That removes your profile, your games and your answers from Supabase, and removes the copy on the phone. If you have Smart Ass Pro, the App Store subscription keeps renewing until you cancel it. Cancelling is in iPhone Settings, under your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the account does not cancel it.

Song packs

The host's voice for each decade is a download the app requests from Apple's App Store servers the first time you play that decade. Apple handles that download; it carries nothing about you.

Purchases

Smart Ass Pro, if you buy it, is an App Store subscription. Apple processes the payment: we never see or store your card. Cancelling works the way every App Store subscription does, in iPhone Settings under your name → Subscriptions. Deleting your account in the app does not cancel the subscription.

To know whether Pro is active, the app uses RevenueCat (revenuecat.com). RevenueCat stores the Pro entitlement: which plan is active and whether it renews. It acts on our behalf.

  • What RevenueCat receives: an app user ID — a random ID while you are signed out, or the id Supabase assigns to your account once you are signed in, so Pro follows that account — plus the app version, device model, iOS version and language, your App Store country and currency, the IP address any internet request carries, and, when you buy or restore, the purchase record Apple issues: which plan, price, trial and renewal dates, and Apple's transaction IDs. It does not receive your name, your email, your nickname, or your card.
  • Why: to check Pro and unlock the decades it includes. It is not used for advertising or tracking.
  • How long: RevenueCat keeps the record while the subscription exists and as long as needed for accounting. You can ask us to delete that record (see Your rights). Deleting it does not cancel the subscription.

RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/. Even if you never buy anything, the app contacts RevenueCat when it starts, to load the Pro offer and check whether Pro is active.

What we deliberately do not collect

  • No tracking. No advertising ID, no ads, no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No location, microphone, camera, contacts or photos. The host talks; the app does not listen.
  • No email address, and no payment card. Your nickname is the only name we store, and only if you sign in. App events do not include it.

Third parties

  • Apple: Apple Music playback, previews and covers, Sign in with Apple, song pack downloads and App Store payments, as described above. Governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Supabase (supabase.com): hosts our database. App events are stored there, and, if you sign in, your profile, finished games and answers. Supabase stores them on our behalf and does not use them for its own purposes.
  • RevenueCat (revenuecat.com): stores the Pro entitlement, as described under Purchases. RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Children

Smart Ass is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

The app events are not linked to you, so we cannot find yours on request; deleting and reinstalling the app starts a new random ID with no link to the old one. Your profile, games and answers are removed when you delete the account in the app (Settings → Player → Delete account). That does not cancel an App Store subscription. To ask about the RevenueCat record of a purchase, write to us with the order ID from Apple's receipt email. For any question about your data, write to apestudios.io@gmail.com.
