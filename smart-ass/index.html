Smart Ass: Music Trivia

The road trip quiz that talks back. Top 100 songs of each decade from Apple Music — stories, quizzes, and a host who knows too much.

  • Songs you know. Facts you don’t.
  • Wrong answers get roasted — lightly
  • Bragging rights for the ride home

What Smart Ass does

Smart Ass is a music trivia app for the car. Pick a decade and it plays the Top 100 songs of those years through Apple Music.

In story mode a host tells you what happened behind each song. In game mode every song comes with a three-answer quiz, and wrong answers get roasted, lightly.

Questions

Who plays while the car is moving?

The passenger. The quiz is tap-only with three answers, built for the other seat.

Where does the music come from?

Songs play through Apple Music. Smart Ass adds the host, the stories and the questions.

Which decades are in it?

The Top 100 songs of each decade, from the 1950s on.

When is it out?

Soon, on iPhone.
