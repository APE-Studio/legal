Bob: Cynical AI Travel Guide

A map, a button, and a narrator who tells you about the places around you with a dry sense of humour. The tour guide who’s over it — and somehow still useful.

  • Places around you, narrated with stand-up timing
  • Postcard world: ink outlines, stamps, card stock
  • Cynical, warm, never punching down at you

What Bob does

Bob is an audio travel guide. Open the map and every pin is a place with a story. Tap one and Bob tells you what really happened there, out loud, in about forty seconds.

Short on time? Tell Bob how long you have and he draws a walk from where you’re standing, past the best stories nearby and back again.

He also knows where locals eat and drink, and stamps a passport for every country you’ve heard a story in.

Questions

Which cities does Bob cover?

Guided walks in Tel Aviv, London, Paris, Prague and Rome. Single stories work almost everywhere else.

Do I have to stare at my phone?

No. Pocket it. Bob keeps talking with the screen locked. If you’d rather read along, the words light up as he says them.

Do I need an account?

No. Sign in if you like; guests get the same tour.

What happens to my location?

It is only used to find what’s near you. Bob keeps no history of where you’ve been. No ads, no trackers.

Is Bob free?

Your first stories are free. After that, Bob Weekly or Bob Yearly gives you unlimited stories.
