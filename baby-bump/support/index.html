Support

Bump — APE Studios

Questions, a bug, or something that doesn't feel right? Write to apestudios.io@gmail.com. A person reads every message, usually within two working days. Please mention your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Your photos

  • Where are they? Inside Bump on this phone only. They are not uploaded anywhere, so we cannot see or recover them.
  • New phone? Restore your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) and the album comes back with it. Keep iPhone backup on.
  • A copy in Photos: open a photo and tap Save to Photos, or turn on "Save copies to Photos" in Settings.
  • Delete everything: Settings → Delete journey and photos. Copies you saved to Photos are not touched.

Bump Pro

  • Cancel: on your iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Bump. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • Restore on a new iPhone: sign in with the same Apple ID, open the subscription screen in Bump and tap Restore.
  • Refund: Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Free trial: if you don't want to pay, cancel before the trial ends; the app shows the date it ends.

Reminders

Turn the daily reminder on or off, or change its time, in Bump → Settings. The lock-screen text never mentions pregnancy.
