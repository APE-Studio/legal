Bump: Pregnancy Photo Album

One photo a day, for the whole pregnancy. Private. On your phone. No account, no cloud theatre.

  • See how far you came — one scroll through nine months

  • Yours alone: no account, no cloud

What Bump does

Bump is a pregnancy photo album for iPhone. A daily nudge, one tap, one photo: bump, face, feet, whatever you want to remember.

Weeks blur together. One photo a day is small enough to keep up with, and nine months later it adds up to the whole story in a single scroll.

No AI, no filters, no ghost overlays, no ads. Your photos, as taken.

Questions

Where are my photos stored?

In Bump’s private storage on your phone, not in your shared photo library. They leave the device only when you share, save a copy or back up.

Do I need an account?

No. Nothing to sign up for, no email to give.

Does it have to be a bump photo?

No. Any moment counts.

Is Bump free?

The album is free. Bump Pro adds comparing any two days side by side and full-quality export without a watermark.
