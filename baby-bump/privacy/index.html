Bump is a private daily album of your pregnancy: one photo a day, a clock that counts weeks and days, and a way to look back. It runs on iPhone.

The short version: photos stay on this phone. They are not uploaded. They leave only when you tap Share or Save to Photos. There is no account and no sign-in, and no server of ours ever receives a photo, a date, your due date or your name. The app does record how it is used (which screens you open, that you added a photo in week 12, that you made a comparison, and errors) under a random ID that is not linked to you, so we can fix problems and see what to build next — see App events below. If you subscribe to Bump Pro, Apple handles the payment and RevenueCat keeps an anonymous record of it (see Subscriptions below); no photo or date is ever part of either.

Photos on your device

Every photo you take or import in Bump is saved as a file inside the app on this phone. It is not uploaded to us or to anyone else. Our database never receives a photo, and there is no account that could be tied to one.

Your iPhone backup. Because the photos are ordinary app data, they are included when your iPhone backs itself up to iCloud or to a computer, the same as any other app. That backup is between you and Apple; we never see it. Restoring a backup on a new phone brings the album back.

Share and Save to Photos. A photo leaves the app only when you choose to share it or save it. Save to Photos adds a copy to your Photos library; the first time, Bump asks iOS for permission to add photos — it never asks to read or change what is already there. Share opens the normal iPhone share sheet, and the photo goes only where you send it. Bump keeps no record of what you shared or saved and never deletes anything from your Photos library, not when you delete a photo in the app, not when you delete everything, and not when you uninstall.

Importing. When you import a photo from your library, Bump makes its own copy. The original stays in Photos untouched. Importing uses the iOS photo picker, which shows the app only the pictures you choose and needs no permission.

Camera. The camera is used only when you tap to take a photo, and the picture goes straight into the app.

Deleting. Settings → Delete all app photos removes every photo and its dates from this phone. Uninstalling the app does the same. Neither touches photos you exported or your iPhone backup, which follows Apple's own retention.

What else stays on your device

Your pregnancy clock. The date of your last period, your due date, or the week you told the app you are in — whichever you entered — and the day and week the app works out from it. Stored on the phone, used only to label your photos and the home screen.

Your settings, including any reminder time you set.

Reminders, if you turn them on, are scheduled by iOS on the phone itself. Nothing is sent anywhere to make a reminder fire.

What we collect

No photos, no dates, no name, no email, no account, no advertising. Two things leave the phone: the app events described next, and the subscription record described under Subscriptions.

How the app is used (app events)

While you use Bump, the app writes short records of what happens in it and sends them, in small batches, to our database. If you are offline they wait on the phone and are sent later. Each record holds:

  • What happened: for example "opened the album", "went to the next intro card", "added 3 photos from the library, weeks 10 to 12", "deleted a photo in week 14", "switched the album to weeks", "made a comparison 8 weeks apart", "turned reminders on", "opened the app from a reminder", "opened the subscription screen", "a purchase or restore of Bump Pro went through or failed" (the plan and the result, never payment details). Weeks are pregnancy week numbers; the rough stage you pick in the intro (for example "second trimester") and how you set the clock (last period, due date or weeks now) are recorded, never the date itself.
  • Errors: where something went wrong in the app and a short error code (never the error text, which can contain file names).
  • Technical context: the app version and build, the iOS version, the iPhone model, the time, and two random IDs the app makes itself: one for this install of the app and one for each time you open it.

These records never contain a photo, any part of a photo, a file name, your last-period date, your due date, your name, your email, your location or anything you type. The random install ID is not linked to you or to any other ID (not to RevenueCat's ID either), and it changes if you delete and reinstall the app. Like any request on the internet, sending them shows our server your IP address; we do not store it with the records.

Why: to find and fix problems and to see which parts of the app people use (for example whether people come back for a second weekly photo), so we know what to improve. We do not use them for advertising, we do not sell them, and we do not combine them with data from other companies.

Where and how long: in our database at Supabase, in the European Union (Frankfurt), for up to two years, then they are deleted.

Subscriptions (Bump Pro)

Bump Pro is optional. Apple takes the payment through your Apple ID; we never see your card or your Apple ID. To show the prices, check whether Pro is active and restore it on a new phone, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service that acts on our behalf.

  • What RevenueCat receives: a random ID the app creates on this phone (it is not your name, email or Apple ID), the app version, device model, iOS version and language, your App Store country and currency, the IP address any internet request carries, and — when you buy or restore — the purchase record Apple issues: which plan, price, trial and renewal dates, and Apple's transaction IDs.
  • What it is used for: unlocking Pro, restoring it, and our own anonymous sales totals. Not for advertising, not for tracking you across apps, not sold.
  • Linked to you? No. We have no account system, so the random ID cannot be tied to who you are.
  • How long: RevenueCat keeps the record while the subscription exists and as long as needed for accounting; you can ask us to delete it (see Your rights).

RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/. Even if you never buy anything, the app contacts RevenueCat when it starts and when the subscription screen opens, to get the prices and check whether Pro is active, using the same random ID.

What we deliberately do not do

  • No upload of photos or dates. There is no cloud copy of your album on our side, ever.
  • No automatic saving to your Camera Roll. A photo reaches your Photos library only when you tap Save to Photos on it.
  • No iCloud Photos as the source of truth. The app reads its own files, not your library.
  • No Files app sharing. The album folder is not exposed to the Files app or to other apps.
  • No dates, names or photos in app events. Only week numbers, counts and which button or screen.
  • No advertising, no tracking, no advertising ID, no data brokers.
  • No health data. The app does not read or write Apple Health.

Permissions

  • Camera — asked the first time you tap to take a photo, never at launch. If you decline, you can still import photos.
  • Add to Photos — asked the first time you tap Save to Photos. This is Apple's add-only permission: the app can add pictures and cannot see or change your library. Share needs no permission.
  • Read your Photos — never asked. Importing uses the iOS picker, which needs no permission.
  • Notifications — asked only if you set a daily reminder. Declining just means no reminder.

Third parties

Apple provides the phone, the camera, the Photos library, the backup and the App Store, under Apple's privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). RevenueCat handles the subscription record, as described above (https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/). Supabase (https://supabase.com/privacy) hosts our database, where the app events are stored; it stores them on our behalf and does not use them for its own purposes. No other company is involved. The only network requests the app makes are to Apple and RevenueCat, for the subscription, and to our Supabase database, for the app events.

Children

Bump is made for adults who are pregnant. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Your photos and dates are on your phone and under your control. Delete a single photo in the app, delete the whole album from Settings, or uninstall. The app events are not linked to you, so we cannot find yours on request; deleting and reinstalling the app starts a new random ID with no link to the old one. The only other thing held off your phone is the anonymous subscription record above. Because it carries no name or email, we can only find yours from the order ID in Apple's receipt email: send it to us and we will ask RevenueCat to show or delete that record (deleting it does not cancel the subscription — Apple handles that). There is nothing else on our side that we could find, export, correct or erase. You are always welcome to write to apestudios.io@gmail.com with any question.
