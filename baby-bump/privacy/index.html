Baby Bump Tracker is a private daily album of your pregnancy: one photo a day, a clock that counts weeks and days, and a way to look back. It runs on iPhone.

The short version: photos stay on this phone. They are not uploaded. They leave only when you tap Export. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server of ours that ever receives a photo, a date or anything else about you.

Photos on your device

Every photo you take or import in Baby Bump Tracker is saved as a file inside the app on this phone. It is not uploaded to us or to anyone else. We have no server that could receive it, and no account that could be tied to it.

Your iPhone backup. Because the photos are ordinary app data, they are included when your iPhone backs itself up to iCloud or to a computer, the same as any other app. That backup is between you and Apple; we never see it. Restoring a backup on a new phone brings the album back.

Export. A photo leaves the app only when you tap Export on it and choose what to do. Save to Photos adds a copy to your Photos library; the first time, Baby Bump Tracker asks iOS for permission to add photos — it never asks to read or change what is already there. Share opens the normal iPhone share sheet, and the photo goes only where you send it. Baby Bump Tracker keeps no record of what you exported and never deletes anything from your Photos library, not when you delete a photo in the app, not when you delete everything, and not when you uninstall.

Importing. When you import a photo from your library, Baby Bump Tracker makes its own copy. The original stays in Photos untouched. Importing uses the iOS photo picker, which shows the app only the pictures you choose and needs no permission.

Camera. The camera is used only when you tap to take a photo, and the picture goes straight into the app.

Deleting. Settings → Delete all app photos removes every photo and its dates from this phone. Uninstalling the app does the same. Neither touches photos you exported or your iPhone backup, which follows Apple's own retention.

What else stays on your device

Your pregnancy clock. The date of your last period, your due date, or the week you told the app you are in — whichever you entered — and the day and week the app works out from it. Stored on the phone, used only to label your photos and the home screen.

Your settings, including any reminder time you set.

Reminders, if you turn them on, are scheduled by iOS on the phone itself. Nothing is sent anywhere to make a reminder fire.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no account. It does not contact our servers because it has none to contact. If a future version adds any of this, this page will say exactly what and why before it ships.

What we deliberately do not do

  • No upload of photos, dates or anything else. There is no cloud copy of your album on our side, ever.
  • No automatic saving to your Camera Roll. A photo reaches your Photos library only when you tap Save to Photos on it.
  • No iCloud Photos as the source of truth. The app reads its own files, not your library.
  • No Files app sharing. The album folder is not exposed to the Files app or to other apps.
  • No advertising, no tracking, no advertising ID, no data brokers.
  • No health data. The app does not read or write Apple Health.

Permissions

  • Camera — asked the first time you tap to take a photo, never at launch. If you decline, you can still import photos.
  • Add to Photos — asked the first time you tap Save to Photos. This is Apple's add-only permission: the app can add pictures and cannot see or change your library. Share needs no permission.
  • Read your Photos — never asked. Importing uses the iOS picker, which needs no permission.
  • Notifications — asked only if you set a daily reminder. Declining just means no reminder.

Third parties

Apple provides the phone, the camera, the Photos library, the backup and the App Store, under Apple's privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). No other company is involved. The app makes no network requests of its own.

Children

Baby Bump Tracker is made for adults who are pregnant. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Everything the app holds is on your phone and under your control. Delete a single photo in the app, delete the whole album from Settings, or uninstall. We hold nothing on our side to export, correct or erase, so a request to us would come back with exactly that answer — but you are welcome to write to apestudios.io@gmail.com with any question.

Changes
