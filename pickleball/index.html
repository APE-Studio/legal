Pickleball Score Watch

Score on your wrist. Phone in the bag. An Apple Watch scorer for pickleball, with a thin iPhone companion when you need it.

  • Lime = serving side — readable in sunlight
  • Callout score is the hero everywhere
  • Live Activities when the phone stays pocketed

What Pickleball Score Watch does

Pickleball Score Watch is a score keeper for Apple Watch. One tap per rally and the watch tracks the score, who is serving and the server number, so the call is always right: 0-0-2 to open, 9-4-2 near the end.

The serving side is always lime, readable in sunlight, with a game timer and haptics alongside.

The phone stays in the bag. A light iPhone companion keeps your match history, stats and settings, and shows a Live Activity while you play.

Questions

Does it score doubles and singles?

Yes. Doubles side-out scoring with the three-number call, singles side-out, and rally scoring.

Can I play to 15 or 21?

Yes. Games go to 11, 15 or 21, with win-by-two on or off.

Do I need my iPhone on court?

No. Scoring runs on the watch. You can also start and score a game from the phone when the watch isn’t around.

Do I need an account?

No accounts, and no ads.

When is it out?

Soon, on the App Store.
