Offroad Gauges displays tilt, heading, speed and altitude derived from your phone's own sensors and its GPS receiver. Those readings are indicative only.   • Do not use the app to decide whether a manoeuvre is safe. The colour bands on the dials are a general guide to steepening angles, not a measurement of your vehicle's rollover threshold, which depends on the vehicle, its load, its tyres and the surface.
