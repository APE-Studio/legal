Offroad Gauges

Built for the trail. Live pitch and roll on big dials, for 4x4 drivers, overlanders, and anyone who leaves the tarmac.

  • Know your angle: the danger zone is printed on the dial
  • Your trail, drawn live on the map
  • Press REC and it keeps your route and your steepest climb

What Offroad Gauges does

Halfway up, it gets steep. Offroad Gauges turns your phone into a pitch and roll inclinometer. It shows your vehicle’s front-to-back and side-to-side tilt in real time, with big needles and the danger zone printed on the dial.

Press REC and the drive is kept: distance, top speed, steepest climb, steepest descent, hardest roll, and your route on a map. Back at camp, the trip becomes a share card you can post, and the hard climbs earn you stickers.

Also inside: a live trail map, a GPS compass, speed and altitude, a sunset countdown and a landscape gauge cluster.

Questions

How do I calibrate the inclinometer?

Mount your phone at any angle and tap SET LEVEL once. From then on the gauges read your vehicle, not your phone.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The gauges and the trip recorder need no signal at all.

Which vehicles does it work with?

Any SUV, truck or 4x4.

Is Offroad Gauges free?

Every gauge is free, and three saved trips are free forever. Offroad Gauges Pro keeps every drive.
