Privacy Policy

Offroad Gauges — APE Studios

Effective date: 20 August 2026 Developer: APE Studios Contact: isabellmaister@gmail.com

Offroad Gauges is a gauge cluster for off-road driving: tilt dials, compass, speed, altitude, and optional trip recording. This policy describes what the app does with your data.

The short version: your location and your recorded trips stay on your device. We do collect anonymous information about the app itself — that it was installed, what kind of phone it is on, and which screens get used — so we can tell whether the app works. None of that identifies you, and none of it is your location.

What stays on your device

Precise location (GPS). Used while the app is open to display speed, altitude, and heading, and — only when you start a recording — to build the route of a trip. Also used to work out the sun's position for the sunset readout and the automatic day/night theme.

Your trips never leave the phone. Recorded trips, including their routes, are written to the app's own private storage. We cannot see them, and there is no copy anywhere else.

Your settings never leave the phone, including the approximate last position the sunset calculation needs.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. Individual trips can be deleted at any time from the Trips tab.

What we collect

All of this is about the app, not about you. It is sent over an encrypted connection to our own database and to Google Analytics for Firebase.

An anonymous install identifier. When you first open the app it signs itself in anonymously and receives a random identifier. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number, Google account, or advertising ID — we cannot use it to work out who you are, and neither can anyone else. It exists so that two app openings can be recognised as the same install rather than two different ones. It is created and destroyed with the install: uninstalling the app ends it permanently.

Basic device and app information, attached to that identifier: your phone's model, its Android version, the version of Offroad Gauges you are running, your language and region, and your Offroad Gauges settings (metric or imperial, day or night theme).

How the app is used. Anonymous events recording which screens you reach and which actions you take — for example that a trip was recorded, that the Pro screen was shown, or that a trophy was earned. These carry no coordinates, no route data, and no trip names.

Whether you subscribed, as a yes/no. Payment details are handled entirely by Google Play and never reach us.

What we deliberately do not collect

  • Your location, to us. No coordinates, no routes, and no trip data are ever sent to our database, and none of it goes into analytics, advertising, or any data broker. The one exception is unavoidable and is not us: to draw a map, the Google Maps SDK must ask Google for the map of the area you are looking at, and on the Map tab that area is where you are. See Google Maps SDK below. Turning to a different tab stops it; nothing about the gauges, the sunset readout, or trip recording needs it.
  • Your identity. No account, no email address, no name, no phone number, no contacts.
  • Your advertising ID, and no advertising or tracking networks. Offroad Gauges shows no ads and does not sell or share data with data brokers.
  • Your photos, files, microphone, or camera.

Third parties

Four Google services are involved. Their handling of the data described here is governed by Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — receives the anonymous usage events and device information described above. It assigns its own anonymous app instance identifier, which is likewise created and destroyed with the install.
  • Google Maps SDK — draws the Map tab, the map behind trip cards, and the map on a shared trip image. Drawing a map means asking Google for that map, so the area being viewed is sent to Google. On the Map tab that area is centred on you, so this amounts to your approximate position; behind a trip card it is the area that trip covered. This is Google receiving it in order to render tiles, not us collecting it — we get nothing back.
  • Google Play Billing — processes Pro subscriptions and the one-time purchase. Payment details are handled entirely by Google Play; the app never sees or stores payment information.
  • Google Play — if you tap the rate prompt, it opens the app's Play Store listing.

Our own database is hosted by Supabase (supabase.com), which stores the install record described above on our behalf and does not use it for its own purposes.

Sharing a trip

Sharing a trip card creates an image containing that trip's route and statistics and hands it to the app you choose. That image goes wherever you send it. Nothing is shared unless you tap Share and pick a destination, and no copy reaches us.

Permissions

  • Location (precise) — the gauges and trip recording, as described above. Your position is never transmitted to us. It reaches Google only as the viewport of a map you are looking at.
  • Foreground service (location) — keeps a recording running while the screen is off during a drive. The app does not request background location access, and does not record when you have not started a trip.
  • Notifications — shows the ongoing notification while a trip is recording. Declining it only hides that notification.
  • Internet — required by the Google and Supabase services listed above.

Children

Offroad Gauges is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Your trips, routes, and settings are yours alone and never reach us, so there is nothing on our side to export or correct — delete individual trips in the app, or uninstall to remove everything.

For the anonymous install record we do hold:

  • Deleting the app deletes it. Uninstalling ends the anonymous identifier permanently. We keep no way to link a future install to a past one.
  • You can ask us to erase it sooner. Email isabellmaister@gmail.com from the device in question and we will delete the record and the identifier behind it.

Because the identifier is anonymous by design, we may be unable to locate a specific record from an email alone — which is a consequence of collecting as little as we do, not a refusal.

Data retention

Install records are kept while the install exists and for up to 24 months after it stops being seen, then deleted. Analytics events follow Google's retention settings for Analytics for Firebase.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated effective date, and, where required, announced in the app's Play Store listing.
